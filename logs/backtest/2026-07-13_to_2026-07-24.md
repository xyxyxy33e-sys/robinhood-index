# Backtest — 0DTE Strategy, 2026-07-13 → 2026-07-24 (10 trading days)

Method: real Robinhood market data throughout. Underlying scored from 5-minute
bars (pre-market 9:00 ET onward); entries evaluated at 9:45/10:00/10:30/11:00/11:30
checkpoints (approximation of the live 3-minute re-check cadence); every triggered
trade replayed against the ACTUAL price history of the specific 0DTE contract the
playbook would have selected (10-minute bars for expired contracts — the finest
real granularity Robinhood serves; 5-minute for 7/24). Current config sizing:
$500 max premium/position, ≤3 concurrent (per-symbol, mixed directions), ≤4
entries/day, stop −30% (exit modeled at exactly −30%), TP +60% (exit at exactly
+60%), hard close ~13:25 ET. Fees (~$0.08/contract round trip, ≈$23 total) and
slippage NOT modeled.

## Key findings

1. **First-checkpoint (9:45) entries were the weak spot**: 10 trades, 2 wins,
   net −$523. Opening-momentum signals repeatedly fired at the exact morning
   extreme and reversed (7/14, 7/17, 7/20 are pure examples). Entries from 10:00
   onward: 22 trades, 12 wins, **net +$1,854**. Consider `entry_earliest: "10:00"`.
2. **Mixed-direction books earned their keep**: 7/16 (SPY/QQQ puts + IWM calls)
   and 7/22 (SPY calls + IWM puts) both saw the two sides diverge as designed.
3. **Trend days pay for everything**: 7/23 + 7/24 (+$1,276 combined) were
   sustained directional days where re-entries compounded; the entry cap (4/day)
   bound gains on 7/23 when all three symbols still qualified at 11:30.
4. **Fill sensitivity is real**: three trades were decided by less than 2 cents
   (7/13 #2, 7/21 #2, 7/22 #2 — the last escaped its stop by $0.0004). With
   real spreads/slippage these outcomes flip; treat the net figure as having wide
   error bars, plausibly ±$400.
5. **Sizing edge case**: cheap late-morning contracts produce big lots (50×$0.10
   on 7/15, 31×$0.16 on 7/16). Consider a premium floor (e.g. min $0.25) to avoid
   spread-dominated fills.

## Caveats
Ten days is not statistical significance. The window skewed favorable (two strong
trend days at the end). Checkpoint entries under-sample the live 3-min cadence.
Exits are modeled at exact ±30/60% rather than bar prints. Expired-contract data
is 10-minute OHLC; intra-bar stop-vs-TP ordering was resolved via the underlying's
5-minute path (conservative: stop-first when ambiguous). VIX regime filter assumed
passing (VIX stayed in the teens all window). No commissions/fees/slippage.
